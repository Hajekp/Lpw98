Voice commands

Filling in a headset

"12"headset number
"next" / "back"move between fields
"now"upload start or finish, saves
"done" / "save row"save and start the next

Getting around

"data size"jump to a field
"headset 12"reopen a headset
"headset 12 hours"reopen at a field

Problems

"problems" → "12" → notethen "next"
"add" / "back"add more, or cancel

Developer

"developer" → "12"headset number, then "next"
"5" → "next"dev mode episodes
logs → "next" / "done"saves the developer row
"back" / "cancel"go back, or leave without saving

Checking

"live count"what's uploading
"read back"hear the row
"stop listening"mic off

To fix a value, just say the right one. More detail is in How to use.
